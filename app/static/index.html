Physics Formulas & Equations

Einstein field equation · theory context Gμν + Λgμν = (8πG/c⁴)Tμν

Curvature couples to stress-energy. This app does not solve the full 3+1 equation: it uses exact Schwarzschild/Kerr diagnostics, linear Schwarzschild perturbations, and a phenomenological binary waveform.

AP Physics C · vectors and gravitation F⃗₁₂ = Gm₁m₂ r⃗/r³;   a⃗₁ = F⃗₁₂/m₁

The mechanics panel decomposes separation, relative velocity, force, acceleration, momentum and angular momentum into SI components at the selected track sample.

AP Physics C · impact and energy J⃗ = Δp⃗;   ΔK = ½μv²n(1−e²)

For finite-size Newtonian impacts, restitution e sets normal rebound speed. Lost translational kinetic energy is reported as impact dissipation; repeated low-e contacts settle into a merged-sphere proxy. Fragmentation, heat transport and material strength are not modeled.

AP Physics C · bound orbit and contact vcirc = √(GM/r);   T = 2π√(r³/GM)

The orbit control initializes a bound apocenter with a chosen fraction of circular speed. Newtonian gravity is conservative between impacts, so only an orbit whose periapsis reaches the finite-radius bodies collides; no invisible drag is added.

Regge–Wheeler / Zerilli master equation ∂²tΨ − ∂²r*Ψ + Vℓ(r)Ψ = SNL

A 1+1D wave equation for linearized metric perturbations on a fixed Schwarzschild background. The optional λΨ² term is a controlled proxy, not a full nonlinear Einstein source.

Newtonian acceleration · collision/scattering/orbit r̈ = −G(m₁+m₂) r⃗ / |r⃗|³

Inputs are converted to total-mass geometric units; the relative orbit is integrated with adaptive DOP853 and a terminal surface-contact event. Restitution controls impact response. Black-hole contact uses an absorbing Schwarzschild-radius proxy with a qualitative fade, not relativistic horizon dynamics or a numerical-relativity merger.

Energy and angular momentum E = ½μv² − Gm₁m₂/r;   L⃗ = μ(r⃗ × v⃗)

For an isolated Newtonian pair these are conserved, including through an ideal elastic contact. The plots report energy as E/(Mc²) and angular momentum as c|L|/(GM²).

Leading-order quadrupole strain h+ ≈ [G/(c⁴D)] d²(Ixx−Iyy)/dt²

The Newtonian tracks produce a diagnostic far-field strain at distance D; h× uses 2Ixy. Finite differences estimate the second derivative. Radiation reaction is not applied to the orbit.

Schwarzschild curvature invariant K = RαβγδRαβγδ = 48M²/r⁶   (G=c=1)

Exact for one nonrotating black hole. The two-center path diagnostic sums isolated-source terms and is therefore explicitly approximate; K has units M⁻⁴.

Frequency spectrum h̃(f) = ∫ h(t)e−2πiftdt;   Δf ≈ 1/T

The discrete Fourier transform reveals signal power by frequency. The spectrogram repeats a windowed transform over time to expose chirp and ringdown structure.

Curved-spacetime motion d²xμ/dλ² + Γμαβ(dxα/dλ)(dxβ/dλ) = 0

Geodesics are free-fall/light paths in a metric. The lensing renderer visualizes a Schwarzschild-inspired null-ray model; its color and grid are not numerical-relativity data.
